How the plan decides (the tested rules)
  • IV rule: ATM IV of the weekly expiry with 1–4 trading days left, compared at 09:30 / 12:00 / 14:30 with the 60th percentile of the past 500 days at that time. At or above = green; above the 70th percentile = strong. Entries only in the check windows 09:31–09:45, 12:01–12:15, 14:31–14:45 — each reading waits for the check minute's 1-min bar to close, exactly as in the backtest.
  • Setup A (green): sell that expiry — straddle at ATM or strangle at 0.15 delta — and hold to expiry.
  • Setup B (green at 09:30 and the current expiry has 1 day left): sell next week's expiry instead, and exit when the IV rule reads normal at a later check; otherwise hold to expiry.
  • Normal IV / expiry day: no new trade. Put skew steep: the put side did better — lean the strangle toward puts.
  • Size (test): A positions overlap — up to 6 open at once — so one "lot" of A needs about 6 lots of margin; B at most 2. Capital per lot and drawdowns: risk & sizing report.
  • Tested and not used (no edge): Range Diary ideas, Strategy Ideas ranking, OI walls, PCR, FII/Client positions, GEX, VRP (IV − realised vol), forward vol, profit targets, 2× stops, delta rolls. GEX and Vol Surface pages are information only.
Information only — nothing here places an order. Plan trades are followed virtually from 09:30, every 5 minutes while the app runs; worst / best points also use the evening 1-min data.
