How the plan decides

  • IV rule: ATM IV of the weekly expiry with 1–4 trading days left, compared at 09:30 / 12:00 / 14:30 with the 60th percentile of the past 500 days at that time. At or above = green; above the 70th percentile = strong.
  • Setup A (green): sell that expiry — straddle at ATM or strangle at 0.15 delta — and hold to expiry.
  • Setup B (green at 09:30 and the current expiry has 1 day left): sell next week's expiry instead, and exit when the IV rule reads normal at a later check; otherwise hold to expiry.
  • Normal IV / expiry day: no new trade.
  • Put skew steep: the put side did better — lean the strangle toward puts.
  • Size: 1 lot = one straddle. Its worst tested week lost ~₹1.1 lakh — keep the notional within ~2× capital.
  • Not used for decisions (tested, no edge): Range Diary ideas, the Strategy Ideas table ranking, OI walls, PCR, FII/Client positions, profit targets, 2× stops, delta rolls. They stay in the app for reference only.
Information only — nothing here places an order. The plan's trades are followed virtually from their first appearance (from 09:30, every 5 minutes while the app runs).
