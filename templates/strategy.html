Prices: SELL legs at the bid, BUY legs at the ask. Max profit / loss and breakevens are at expiry, after round-trip charges. POP (model) = chance NIFTY expires in the profit zone under today's ATM implied volatility (lognormal). POP (history) = the same using NIFTY's real moves over the same number of trading minutes (5 years of 1-min data). EV = average P&L over those outcomes. 5-yr record = this exact template (same strike rule) entered at the same days-to-expiry and time of day on every week of the last 5 years, on real historical option prices: win % · average · worst, held to expiry (1 lot, after slippage & charges). "At similar IV" keeps only the quarter of those weeks whose ATM implied volatility at entry was closest to today's — selling pays far more in high-IV weeks, buying only in low-IV ones.
