Rules (your method)

  1. Cycle start day (after expiry, usually Wednesday): after the first 5-min candle, the highest-volume 100-pt CALL and PUT strikes are the leaders.
  2. Upper = CE leader + PE leader's first-candle high · Lower = PE leader − CE leader's first-candle high.
  3. Sell the round strike nearest Upper (CE) and nearest Lower (PE), 1 lot each, no hedge.
  4. Leaders tracked on each day's own volume; when a leader changes, the range is recomputed with that day's first-candle highs.
  5. Call rolls down when the range's call strike falls; put rolls up when the range's put strike rises. A leg moves away only if it is in the money. No rolls on expiry day.
  6. Exit all: loss reaches 50% of entry premium · net profit reaches 70% of entry premium · 15:00 on the day before expiry (expiry day if that is a holiday).

Backtest — every completed cycle with recorded data (same code as live, 5-min exchange candles, ₹0.5 slippage per order, charges included)
