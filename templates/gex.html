How this is computed

Nearest weekly expiry (or the one chosen), strikes within ±8% of NIFTY. Gamma = Black-76 on the put-call-parity forward with the IV of the out-of-the-money option at each strike. Net GEX = Σ gamma × (call OI − put OI) × NIFTY² × 1%, in ₹ crore per 1% NIFTY move, under the usual convention that dealers are long the calls and short the puts customers trade — OI does not show who is really short. Total = the same with call OI + put OI. Today: the live Arrow chain, sampled every 5 minutes while the app runs. Earlier days: the 1-min history, any time from 09:20 to 15:25. Other dashboards use other conventions, so absolute numbers differ between sites.
