How this is computed

IV per strike = Black-76 on the put-call-parity forward, from the out-of-the-money option at that strike, every 5 minutes (09:20–15:25), interpolated onto a 0.25% moneyness grid (−5% … +5%); prices older than 30 minutes are left out. Today: the live Arrow chain, sampled every 5 minutes while the app runs. Earlier days: the 1-min history; the second expiry appears where its file covers that day (from 10 Oct 2026 the evening job fetches next week's expiry every day).
