    AUC: 0.5 = coin toss, 1.0 = perfect. "Top 10%" = how often a break followed when the model was most confident.

    Rule of thumb — chance NIFTY breaks the wall within 60 min

    Distance in ATM straddles (09:30). Moving toward / away = NIFTY moved more than 0.1 straddle toward / away from the wall in the last 15 min. Small print: 2021–23 / 2024–26 rate, and the chance it closes the day beyond the wall.

    Each OI signal on its own, within the same distance band

    Break-in-60-min rate for each fifth of the signal (lowest → highest), inside each distance band so a signal is not credited for simply being closer.

    Method & limits

    • Nearest weekly expiry, 100-pt strikes, 1-min OI and prices from the Fyers history; exchange OI updates every few minutes, so a 1-min OI change can lag.
    • Walls are re-picked at every 5-min snapshot; consecutive snapshots of the same approach are not independent.
    • A break = a 1-min NIFTY close beyond the wall strike.
