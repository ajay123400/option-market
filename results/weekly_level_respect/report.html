Do the week's range levels hold on the following days?

Method

  • Our levels: every UPPER / LOWER the method produced during the week (Day-1 09:20 and each leader-change recalculation), tested from the next day's open to the expiry close — only if NIFTY was still on the right side of it at that day's close.
  • Random: same side and the same kind of distance from the creation-day close, taken from another week. Shifted: our level moved ± pts — if the exact level matters, these should do clearly worse.
  • Touched = within 10 pts · Rejected = moves 60 pts back before breaking · Broke = 5-min close 20+ pts beyond · Fake = broke, then came back 20+ inside later (before expiry) · Held = the expiry close is still on the right side.

    ± = 95% confidence range of the rejection rate. If our row's range overlaps the controls', the difference is not significant.
