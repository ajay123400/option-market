    Method & limits

    • IVs from 1-min closes with Black-76 on the put-call-parity forward; ATM IV = average of the ATM call and put; 25Δ skew = IV of the put nearest 25Δ − IV of the call nearest 25Δ (same expiry).
    • Term = ATM IV of the nearest expiry with ≥ 1 day left ÷ ATM IV of the one after, at 09:30.
    • Strategy results are the 5-year template backtest (same strike rules as the live Ideas table), 09:30 entries 1–4 days before expiry, held to expiry, 1 lot, all charges.
    • High-IV days are fewer and come in clusters (a volatile month gives many of them) — the n are not independent days of evidence.
    • An absolute IV threshold can drift with the market's regime; re-check it each year.
