Positional range method — with vs without the 70% target

~255 weekly expiries, Sep 2021 → Sep 2026 · 1 lot (lot size of that time) · 1-min data · 0.5 pt slippage per order · all charges.

Common rules

EntrySell the 50-pt strikes nearest UPPER / LOWER of the volume-leader range (UPPER = CE leader + PE leader's first 5-min high, LOWER = PE leader − CE leader's first 5-min high).Range & rollsRange recalculated only on a leader change (5-min checks). Call rolls down to the strike nearest the new UPPER, put rolls up to the strike nearest the new LOWER; never away; at most 100 pts past a straddle.ExitTarget: net P&L after charges ≥ 70% of the entry premium. Otherwise held to expiry and settled vs NIFTY's close.Width filterNo entry while the range is narrow. Day-1 only: trade the week only if the Day-1 09:20 range is at least 350 / 400 pts wide. Late entry: otherwise keep watching; enter on the first leader change (Day 1 or any later day) whose new range is at least 350 / 400 wide — up to the day before expiry (or, "incl. expiry day", on expiry day too). No hedge, no stop loss.TargetEither exit at +70% of the entry premium (net of charges), or no target: every week is held to expiry and settled vs NIFTY's close (rolls unchanged).

What the test says

  • Enter on first range >= 400 (Day 1 or later, till day before expiry) · no hedge · no SL: 154 weeks traded, net ₹305,281 (2021–23 ₹123,122, 2024–26 ₹182,159), win 74.0%, max drawdown -₹46,589.
  • Enter on first range >= 350 (Day 1 or later, till day before expiry) · no hedge · no SL: 193 weeks traded, net ₹326,377 (2021–23 ₹107,207, 2024–26 ₹219,170), win 71.5%, max drawdown -₹64,609.
  • Enter only if Day-1 09:20 range >= 400 · no hedge · no SL: 97 weeks traded, net ₹199,804 (2021–23 ₹83,307, 2024–26 ₹116,497), win 74.2%, max drawdown -₹52,558.
  • Late entries work too: with the 400 filter, 57 weeks were entered after Day-1 09:20 (a later leader change widened the range) — net ₹105,477, win 74%. Most came on Day 1 itself; entries only 3–4 days before expiry added little.
  • For comparison, trading every week (no filter) made ₹219,406 with a -₹101,186 drawdown: the narrow weeks the filter skips were, on balance, losing weeks.
  • Caution: the 350 / 400 thresholds come from looking at this same data, the worst week (−₹36,755) is unchanged because it was a wide-range week, and naked positions still carry crash risk this 5-year sample does not contain.
  • What holding does to the target weeks (≥ 400 late entry): 103 weeks hit the 70% target. Held to expiry instead, 67 ended better and 36 worse; 11 of them turned into a loss. Net effect ₹43,404.
