Positional range method — breakeven-based adjustments (A / B / C)

~255 weekly expiries, Sep 2021 → Sep 2026 · 1 lot (lot size of that time) · 1-min data · 0.5 pt slippage per order · all charges.

Common rules

EntrySell the 50-pt strikes nearest UPPER / LOWER of the volume-leader range (UPPER = CE leader + PE leader's first 5-min high, LOWER = PE leader − CE leader's first 5-min high).Range & rollsRange recalculated only on a leader change (5-min checks). Call rolls down to the strike nearest the new UPPER, put rolls up to the strike nearest the new LOWER; never away; at most 100 pts past a straddle.ExitTarget: net P&L after charges ≥ 70% of the entry premium. Otherwise held to expiry and settled vs NIFTY's close.Width filterNo entry while the range is narrow. Day-1 only: trade the week only if the Day-1 09:20 range is at least 350 / 400 pts wide. Late entry: otherwise keep watching; enter on the first leader change (Day 1 or any later day) whose new range is at least 350 / 400 wide — up to the day before expiry (or, "incl. expiry day", on expiry day too). No hedge, no stop loss.A · centredSell a straddle at the 50-pt strike nearest the range middle; when a recalculated range's middle is 50+ (or 100+) pts from the straddle, move both legs to the new middle. B · guardCurrent strikes (nearest UPPER / LOWER) and the usual roll toward the market; in addition, when a range edge comes within 50 (or 100) pts of that side's breakeven, roll that leg AWAY until the breakeven is 50 (100) pts beyond the edge again. Breakeven counts booked P&L. C · balancedSell the CE/PE pair whose breakevens are equally far outside the range; re-balance on every range change. ExitTarget 90% of the entry premium (net of charges), otherwise held to expiry. No stop, no hedge.

What the test says

  • Enter on first range >= 400 (Day 1 or later, till day before expiry) · no hedge · no SL: 154 weeks traded, net ₹305,281 (2021–23 ₹123,122, 2024–26 ₹182,159), win 74.0%, max drawdown -₹46,589.
  • Enter on first range >= 350 (Day 1 or later, till day before expiry) · no hedge · no SL: 193 weeks traded, net ₹326,377 (2021–23 ₹107,207, 2024–26 ₹219,170), win 71.5%, max drawdown -₹64,609.
  • Enter only if Day-1 09:20 range >= 400 · no hedge · no SL: 97 weeks traded, net ₹199,804 (2021–23 ₹83,307, 2024–26 ₹116,497), win 74.2%, max drawdown -₹52,558.
  • Late entries work too: with the 400 filter, 57 weeks were entered after Day-1 09:20 (a later leader change widened the range) — net ₹105,477, win 74%. Most came on Day 1 itself; entries only 3–4 days before expiry added little.
  • For comparison, trading every week (no filter) made ₹219,406 with a -₹101,186 drawdown: the narrow weeks the filter skips were, on balance, losing weeks.
  • Caution: the 350 / 400 thresholds come from looking at this same data, the worst week (−₹36,755) is unchanged because it was a wide-range week, and naked positions still carry crash risk this 5-year sample does not contain.
  • With the ≥ 400 filter, the best in 2021–23 alone was Current best: range strikes + toward-rolls · ≥400 late entry (₹125,722); its 2024–26 result: ₹234,053. Best over the whole period: Current best: range strikes + toward-rolls · ≥400 late entry (₹359,775).
  • Rolls per week / charges: Current best: range strikes + toward-rolls · ≥400 late entry: 4.55 rolls, ₹50,670 · B · breakeven guard 50: 24.73 rolls, ₹202,376 · B · breakeven guard 100: 32.67 rolls, ₹261,813 · A · centred straddle, move on 50+ shift: 23.75 rolls, ₹231,146 · A · centred straddle, move on 100+ shift: 13.06 rolls, ₹134,787 · C · balanced strangle: 25.25 rolls, ₹232,201.
  • The June 2025 trend week (expiry 26 Jun 2025, NIFTY 24,860 at entry → 25,530 at expiry): Current best: range strikes + toward-rolls · ≥400 late entry: -₹13,032 · B · breakeven guard 50: -₹573 · B · breakeven guard 100: -₹5,002 · A · centred straddle, move on 50+ shift: -₹7,653 · A · centred straddle, move on 100+ shift: -₹3,389 · C · balanced strangle: -₹3,469.
