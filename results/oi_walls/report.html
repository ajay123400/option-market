Do OI walls act as support / resistance?

What the data says

    Test 1 — walls vs other strikes at the same distance

    Wall:

    "Expected" = how many walls would have been broken if they behaved like the other 100-pt strikes at the same distance (in ATM straddles) — fewer actual breaks than expected would mean the wall holds. Broken = NIFTY closes beyond it (rest of day: at 15:30; to expiry: expiry close).

    By distance from NIFTY (in ATM straddles)

    Touch = NIFTY reached the level. Held after touch = of the touches, the share that closed back on the original side.

    Test 5 — selling the wall strike vs a strike of the same delta

    Days 1–4 before expiry, 09:30. Only options traded in the 09:30 minute; sold at the 09:31 close − 0.5 pt; held to expiry. "Same-delta P&L" = a straight line through the 4 non-wall strikes nearest in delta that day, read at the wall's exact delta.

    Method & limits

    • Walls from 09:30 OI of the nearest weekly expiry, 100-pt strikes on the out-of-the-money side, within 3.2 ATM straddles of NIFTY. ΔOI = OI now − OI at the previous day's close.
    • OI does not say who is short: a call wall can be writers or buyers. The test only asks whether the level behaves differently.
    • Levels of the same day are not independent, and a big wall and its neighbours share the same day's move.
