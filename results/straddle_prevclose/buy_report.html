Buying the straddle at yesterday's closing price

Same 1,231 days and data as the selling test · 1-min option prices · 0.5 pt slippage per order unless stated · all charges · 1 lot (65).

Rule

Strike50-pt strike nearest yesterday's NIFTY close (nearest weekly expiry). EntryBuy-stop at yesterday's closing straddle price: bought when the combined price reaches it (a gap open above it buys at the open), 09:15–14:30. ExitSame day, clock time; stop = premium down 20/30/40%; targets +20% to +100% also tried (${N} variants in all).

Verdict

    The key test — how fast the edge disappears after the open

    Gap-open days only. The profit exists only if you buy at the very first 09:15 print; buying at the 1-min close a few minutes later removes it.

    Gap-open days by gap size (exit 09:45, SL 20%, bought at the open print)

    All triggered days, no stop — by exit time

    Slippage sensitivity (gap > 100, open print, exit 09:45)

    Slippage only moves the price by whole points; it cannot model an opening print that simply is not available to fill at.
